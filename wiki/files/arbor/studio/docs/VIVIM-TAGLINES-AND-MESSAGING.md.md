```markdown
# VIVIM — Taglines and Messaging Principles

Status: messaging documentation. Strategic direction, not a claim of completed runtime functionality.

## Established product lines

- **Own your AI · Share your AI · Evolve your AI**
- **My machine. My data. My AI.**
- **Your webapps. Your data. Your AI world.**

## Foundational philosophy — avoid repeated reasoning cost

> The goal isn't to ban LLMs. It's to stop paying the reasoning cost again and again for behavior the software has already learned how to perform.

**Classification:** Foundational product philosophy / long-form positioning statement.

**Meaning:** LLMs remain valuable for novel, ambiguous, complex and not-yet-supported tasks. When a behavior can be expressed as a reliable, reusable, authorized and verified capability, VIVIM should execute that capability without repeatedly invoking model reasoning for the same established work.

**Engineering implications (direction, not shipped-feature claims):**
- Natural language remains the human interface.
- VLang/VEC and the capability SDK can progressively compile, register, verify and reuse learned behaviors.
- Deterministic execution should be preferred where the behavior is known and validated; models remain a fallback for unresolved semantics or genuinely new reasoning.
- Reuse does not bypass owner authorization, effect boundaries, receipts, provenance, revision guards or recovery requirements.
- Measure repeated model calls avoided, cost, latency, reliability, and verification overhead—not just token counts.

**Supporting shorthand (proposed, not replacement slogans):**
- Reason once where possible. Reuse with proof.
- Don't repeatedly reason through what the software already knows how to do.

## Messaging hierarchy

Primary public identity stays **Own your AI · Share your AI · Evolve your AI**. Use the verbatim philosophy line in product vision, architecture narratives, capability-economics explanations, and VLang/VEC positioning. Supporting shorthand is optional draft copy, not an approved brand replacement.
```
